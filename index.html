No. The goal is to create a nutrition approach that supports progress while remaining practical and sustainable.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Your plan can be adapted to a commercial gym, home gym or limited equipment setup.

How quickly will I see results?

Results depend on your starting point, consistency, sleep, nutrition and goal. The program focuses on steady measurable progress rather than unrealistic promises.
